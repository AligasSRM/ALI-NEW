## Approved visual direction
- Premium cinematic dark UI.
- Background: `#0b1220`; panels: `#111c2e` and `#16243a`.
- Accent colors: teal `#5eead4`, coral `#fb8b78`.
- Main text: `#eef4ff`; muted text: `#9aabc3`.
- Responsive desktop/mobile layouts; Arabic RTL and English LTR are planned.
- Preserve the approved baseline. Change it only for a clear technical reason and with Ali's approval.

## Age-gate design scope
The mockup communicates that the platform is intended for adults, may contain sexually explicit content, requires the user to meet 18+ or the higher applicable legal age, offers confirm/exit choices, and advises parents/guardians about parental controls. The notice explicitly is **not identity verification**.
